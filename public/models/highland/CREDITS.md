## Changes made for this application

`source.glb` preserves the original downloaded asset. `highland.glb` is generated by `scripts/prepare-highland.mjs`. The adaptation groups complete connected components into doors, hood, glazing, lights, wheels, seats, display and the remaining body. All **179,692 original triangles** and original vertex attributes are retained. No triangle is sliced and no geometry is invented to split the body. The source authorship and license metadata remain embedded in the adapted GLB.

The model is reoriented to X forward / Y up, normalized to a 4.72 m body length, centered between its axles and grounded. Runtime changes include paint and material response, glass opacity, shadows, selection highlighting, illustrative explosion offsets and animation of detached doors, hood, wheels and steering wheel. The original dashboard and steering wheel are grouped from complete components. The source steering wheel remains on the vehicle's left side (negative Z with X forward and Y up). The aerodynamic pressure colors are an illustrative position-based palette, not CFD or measured pressure.

The source merges the rear deck/trunk lid into the connected main body and does not expose a verified separate charge flap. Those surfaces remain intact. Any separately displayed engineering mechanisms are illustrative procedural additions, not measured manufacturer geometry. Adaptation and animation do not imply endorsement by the original creators or Tesla.
The cabin is presented in its original left-hand-drive geometry. This does not verify all Taiwan-production equipment. Original vertex buffers remain unchanged.
